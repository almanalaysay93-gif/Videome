# Hooks, Type & Impact

## 1. Kinetic hook
**Use/context:** First 1–2 seconds; make the promise or problem visible before viewers scroll. Example: a product appears exactly as the claim snaps into place.
**Build:** Open on a close crop or strong shape; animate one key word/subject from obscured to fully readable in 6–12 frames; hold the message long enough to read; cut into proof/action. Sync one restrained impact or whoosh to the reveal.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Build a first-second opening hook around the brief's single strongest promise. Make the promise and focal subject legible immediately; end on a frame that motivates the next shot rather than adding a second competing hook.

## 2. Gravity drop
**Use/context:** Give a word, product, or icon satisfying weight; useful for a punchline, reveal, or feature landing.
**Build:** Start above frame or just off its final position; use eased acceleration and a short overshoot/bounce; add a contact shadow or scale squash at impact; settle before the next line. Keep physics playful, not destructive, unless requested.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Animate the nominated object/word as if pulled down by gravity, with a clear impact, controlled rebound, and stable readable end state. Tie the landing to the spoken or visual beat.

## 3. Giant captions
**Use/context:** Bold, sound-off-first emphasis for a hook, quote, or one short phrase.
**Build:** Select one phrase, break it into 1–4 words per state; use large high-contrast type, a masked wipe/scale entrance, and brief hold; leave the speaker's face and caption-safe region visible. Keep supporting text small and sparse.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Turn the supplied exact copy into giant, editorial captions with deliberate line breaks and timed emphasis. Preserve every word and speaker visibility; do not add paraphrases.

## 6. Icon strike
**Use/context:** A tiny visual punctuation mark for a feature or claim—e.g., check, bolt, star, or cursor landing beside a benefit.
**Build:** Choose one simple icon with a clear silhouette; strike/scale it into a precise anchor point; add a brief impact accent or stroke; hold as a label/benefit resolves. Use supplied brand icon assets when available.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Build one icon strike that visually punctuates the specified claim. Match the supplied visual language, align it to the copy, and avoid turning the icon into an unrelated logo or decorative swarm.

## 35. Glass type
**Use/context:** Premium, translucent typography over a product, architecture, or beauty shot where depth matters.
**Build:** Use a restrained frosted/glass fill, subtle refraction/highlight and background blur; maintain strong edge contrast or a shadow; animate with a slow reveal rather than a noisy bounce. Keep small copy off busy detail.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Create a premium glass-type title that remains readable over the supplied background. Preserve the real scene beneath it, use subtle depth cues, and provide a fallback contrast treatment if the background makes any letter unclear.

## 36. Pop captions
**Use/context:** Energetic spoken-word/social edits; emphasize a few punch words without making every subtitle jump.
**Build:** Time words to speech; keep a steady baseline and type system; scale/color-pop only selected words on their syllable or stressed word; maintain a readable hold and consistent line breaks. Use transcript timing if available.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Produce accurate speech-synced captions with selective word pops on the strongest stresses. Preserve verbatim dialogue, synchronize to the audio, and keep ordinary words calm so emphasis has hierarchy.

## 40. Like burst
**Use/context:** A beat of audience reaction or social validation, especially after a payoff; avoid implying fabricated metrics.
**Build:** Trigger a small burst of hearts/likes from one tap or focal point; use 3–7 particles with varied scale and short trajectories; stop quickly and return to clean composition. Use actual platform styling only if authorized/supplied.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Add a compact like burst to the specified payoff moment. Use generic or approved symbols, no invented counts or platform UI, and make the burst resolve cleanly within the shot.

## 45. Glitch / VHS hit
**Use/context:** Brief disruption for a reveal, transition, throwback mood, or intentional signal loss.
**Build:** Apply a short, motivated hit (roughly 4–10 frames): RGB separation/scanline/noise or frame tear; snap back to a clean image or reveal the next state; preserve faces and essential copy. Use a longer VHS texture only when the look is meant to persist.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Build a single intentional glitch/VHS accent at the specified transition. Control duration and legibility, preserve key subjects/text, and return to a clean stable state unless the brief explicitly requests sustained tape texture.

# Worlds & Tactile Graphics

## 19. Orb
**Use/context:** A simple hero object for a concept, abstract product benefit, or magical reveal.
**Build:** Design one sphere with a coherent material/light setup; use a slow orbit, internal glow, or surface detail as the meaning-bearing motion; reveal a label/feature only when needed; keep background calm. Avoid generic floating ornament if the orb has no narrative role.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Build one purposeful orb visual that represents the brief's named idea. Match material and palette to the brand, give it a clear movement arc, and connect the reveal to a specific message.

## 20. Couch wall
**Use/context:** Cozy creator/testimonial setting where a person speaks against a distinctive sofa/backdrop wall.
**Build:** Compose sofa and wall as a recognizable set; use a stable medium shot with subject separated from background; add soft practical light and a small amount of depth; preserve real interview audio and natural posture. Do not manufacture endorsement text.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Create or edit a warm couch-wall talking-head setup for the supplied speaker. Preserve authentic speech and appearance, maintain comfortable eye line and lighting, and use only approved testimonial claims.

## 30. Green screen
**Use/context:** Put a presenter or object in front of a controlled explainer background or visual example.
**Build:** Key the supplied green-screen footage; refine spill/edge matte; place a correctly scaled background; match direction, exposure, and shadow; keep presenter away from important background labels. If shooting new footage, request even lighting and avoid green clothing.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Composite the supplied keyed subject into the specified background. Preserve fine hair/edges, match light and scale, check spill and shadows, and never imply the background is a real location if it is a graphic.

## 31. Paper cut
**Use/context:** Handmade, tactile storytelling for a brand with warm, crafty, educational, or whimsical tone.
**Build:** Build layered paper shapes with visible cut edges and soft cast shadows; animate simple slides/folds/peels; limit palette and layer count; use deliberate stop-motion-like beats without jitter unless desired. Ensure text remains legible.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Create a paper-cut animation for the specified idea using a small coherent palette and tactile layered shadows. Make the action explain the message and keep all text/brand marks accurate.

## 32. Doodle roll
**Use/context:** Draw attention through hand-drawn arrows, circles, paths, or annotations over footage.
**Build:** Draw vector strokes on transparent overlay; reveal via trim-path/stroke animation as if drawn by hand; point precisely to relevant objects; erase/fade when the point is made. Keep line weight and color consistent.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Animate a concise doodle overlay that guides the eye to the specified subject or step. Use accurate pointing, preserve the footage, avoid covering captions/faces, and clear the drawings at the end.

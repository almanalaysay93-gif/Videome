# Grade, Film & Editorial Packaging

## 10. Grade slider
**Use/context:** Demonstrate a before/after color correction or let the viewer compare a chosen look.
**Build:** Split the same frame with a wipe/slider; keep exposure and framing matched; move the divider steadily from source to graded image; label before/after accurately; finish on the completed grade. Avoid making the source unnaturally poor for contrast.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Create a true before/after grade-slider comparison from the same shot. Use the actual source and grade, align the divider, label honestly, and end on the finished treatment.

## 11. Eyedropper grade
**Use/context:** Show a palette being sampled from a reference and applied to footage or a design.
**Build:** Animate an eyedropper picking one or two colors from the supplied reference; display a restrained swatch; transition those colors into highlights/shadows or graphics; preserve skin tones and brand fidelity. Treat sampling as a visual metaphor unless technically connected.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Build an eyedropper-grade sequence from the approved reference palette. Show sampled colors clearly, apply a coherent grade without distorting skin/products, and don't claim actual software sampling if it is only illustrative.

## 12. Film look
**Use/context:** Give footage a cohesive analog/cinematic character for a mood-led reel or brand story.
**Build:** Choose a specific stock-era reference (contrast, palette, grain, halation, gate weave); apply consistently across shots; protect skin and highlight detail; keep grain/blur subtle at delivery resolution; test cuts for match. Avoid piling on every vintage artifact.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Apply one coherent film-look treatment across the supplied shots, specifying palette, contrast, grain, and any restrained halation. Preserve detail and shot-to-shot continuity; do not use fake damage unless requested.

## 13. Title card
**Use/context:** Open or close a reel with a deliberate title, episode name, or brand statement.
**Build:** Prioritize one title and one secondary line at most; use supplied typography/brand palette; animate a simple reveal; give adequate reading time; transition in/out cleanly. Respect legal/brand lockups and safe margins.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Build an opening/closing title card using exact supplied wording and approved brand assets. Establish a clear hierarchy, appropriate hold, and clean transition; do not redraw or alter the logo.

## 34. Cover story
**Use/context:** Give a reel an editorial cover/feature-story framing—headline, subject, and teaser details like a magazine cover.
**Build:** Compose a strong portrait/product image with one lead headline and up to a few short teasers; make type hierarchy obvious; animate cover elements into position or pull from cover into moving footage. Use original/authorized masthead and copy; avoid imitating a specific publication's trade dress.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Create an original editorial cover-story treatment for the reel using the specified headline and imagery. Keep hierarchy elegant and legible, avoid copying a real magazine identity, and transition naturally into the story footage.

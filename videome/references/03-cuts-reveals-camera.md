# Cuts, Reveals & Camera Tricks

## 9. Jiggle delete
**Use/context:** Remove a distraction, bad take, or obsolete item with a playful editing gesture.
**Build:** Give the target a brief shake or selection outline; perform one clear delete/snap-out; let the empty/clean state settle; use the cleared space to reveal the next point. Make the visual deletion metaphorical unless actually editing the source.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Build a playful jiggle-delete that removes only the nominated element and exposes the intended clean state. Keep the action short, preserve unrelated content, and do not imply irreversible real-world deletion.

## 22. Frame break
**Use/context:** Make a subject appear to cross a border or break out of a panel for emphasis.
**Build:** Establish a visible frame; mask the foreground subject separately from its background; animate the subject across the boundary while the frame remains; add a small edge interaction/shadow; return to normal framing. Use clean matte edges.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Create a frame-break moment with the specified subject crossing a defined border. Separate foreground/background cleanly, preserve anatomy and scene continuity, and keep the break brief enough to reinforce the message.

## 23. Digital zoom
**Use/context:** Direct attention to a detail in existing footage or a screen recording.
**Build:** Set start/end crops around the same point of interest; animate a smooth scale/pan; keep enough resolution to avoid visible pixelation; pause at the detail, then optionally restore the wide view. Use only one zoom focal target.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Build a controlled digital zoom from the supplied footage/screen to the specified detail. Keep the focal point anchored, avoid excessive enlargement, and provide a readable hold or return to the full context.

## 27. Zoom-through cut
**Use/context:** Connect two related spaces, screens, or ideas by pushing through a matching shape.
**Build:** Find a circular/rectangular shape in shot A; zoom until it fills frame; cut on the fill to a shape/scene in shot B; ease out to reveal the new scene. Match center, scale, color, and motion direction.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Design a zoom-through cut between the named shots using a motivated visual match. Align the cut at the fill frame, preserve the intended subject, and make the new scene understandable on exit.

## 29. Pull-back reveal
**Use/context:** Reveal context around a detail—what looked like a small object becomes part of a larger scene or system.
**Build:** Start on the detail; animate a steady reverse scale/camera pull with parallax layers or a continuous source move; reveal context in stages; stop at a composed wide. Make continuity physically plausible or signal a stylized graphic reveal.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Build a pull-back reveal from the specified detail to its full context. Control the reveal order and ending composition; do not introduce unprovided objects or claims.

## 33. Vertigo
**Use/context:** Create unease or dramatic realization using a dolly-zoom perspective shift.
**Build:** Move the camera toward/away from the subject while zooming oppositely to hold subject size; use stable horizon and background geometry; keep the move short. For static source, use layered depth sparingly; don't fake impossible geometry.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Create a restrained vertigo/dolly-zoom effect around the named subject, preserving subject scale while the background perspective shifts. Use only when it supports the emotion; keep faces/edges natural.

## 41. Whip pan
**Use/context:** Fast energetic transition between actions or locations with a motivated direction.
**Build:** Pan rapidly into motion blur at the end of A; cut at peak blur; begin B with matching direction/blur; resolve quickly to a stable frame. Match speed and exposure so it feels like one move.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Build a directional whip-pan cut between the specified shots. Match movement vectors and blur across the cut, resolve promptly, and do not use a whip when the story needs a calm transition.

## 42. Infinite zoom
**Use/context:** Surreal journey through nested images, maps, screens, or repeating visual worlds.
**Build:** Create a chain of images with a shared center/portal; scale the next layer to fill frame at each cut; vary only a few visual levels; maintain clear color/shape anchors. Use composited stills/2.5D rather than claiming an actual endless camera move.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Build a finite, seamless-feeling infinite-zoom sequence from the supplied assets. Connect each layer through a clear visual match, limit the number of levels, and end on a deliberate destination frame.

## 43. Parallax 2.5D
**Use/context:** Add depth and gentle camera movement to a still photograph, product image, or graphic collage.
**Build:** Separate foreground/midground/background; patch gaps behind cutouts; move layers at different small speeds as camera shifts; keep edges natural and avoid large excursions that reveal missing pixels. Add subtle focus/lighting only if consistent.
**Agent 3 build brief:** Turn the supplied still into a restrained 2.5D parallax move. Preserve identity and composition, conceal layer seams, use small depth offsets, and finish without revealing empty cutout regions.
